Cartes Privacy Notice

Version: August 17, 2026.

This notice describes how personal data required to operate Cartes on the Web and WhatsApp, manage Cartes Plus, and review Word documents is processed.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Develando el Código Masónico. For privacy-related requests, email soporte@develandoelcodigomasonico.com.

2. Data that may be processed

  • Phone number and profile name available through WhatsApp.
  • Content of messages sent to Cartes.
  • Message identifiers, dates, times, and technical operational data.
  • Plan and subscription status, confirmed payments, and question or review counters.
  • Records of acceptance or rejection of terms and authorizations, including their versions and message identifiers.
  • Content of .doc and .docx documents voluntarily submitted for review.
  • Information needed to prevent duplicates, abuse, fraud, or technical errors.

Cartes does not request passwords, verification codes, complete payment-card details, official identity documents, or medical information. Do not include sensitive or confidential data that is unnecessary for your question.

3. Primary purposes

  • Receive and answer questions.
  • Maintain the conversation context needed to provide the service.
  • Manage the free plan, Cartes Plus, renewals, and usage limits.
  • Manage payments and confirm their status through Mercado Pago or PayPal.
  • Process Word documents and generate feedback requested by the user.
  • Record consent, authorizations, and events needed to demonstrate the requested operation.
  • Protect security, prevent duplicates, investigate failures, and provide support.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data is not used to sell personal information or send third-party advertising.

4. Documents submitted for review

Documents are processed exclusively to perform the requested review. The file is held temporarily in memory during processing and is deleted when the attempt ends, whether the review succeeds or fails.

Generated feedback may identify possible issues involving citations, references, or third-party content, but it does not constitute a legal assessment, a certification of originality, or a finding of plagiarism.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed by Mercado Pago or PayPal. Cartes receives operational payment data, such as identifiers, status, amount, and transaction reference, but it does not directly store complete payment-card details.

6. Service providers and necessary transfers

Meta and WhatsApp, Netlify, Mercado Pago, PayPal, and the configured artificial intelligence provider may be involved in operating the service. These providers may process information as processors or independent controllers under their own terms and policies.

Information may also be disclosed when necessary to comply with a legal obligation, respond to a competent authority, or protect the security of the service and its users.

7. Retention

Documents are deleted after each review attempt. Messages, counters, subscription records, consent records, and technical data are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, address inquiries, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable obligations.

When data is no longer necessary, it will be deleted or anonymized according to technical and legal possibilities.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are applied, including environment variables, request validation, access controls, duplicate prevention, and temporary document processing. No Internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

9. Rights of access, rectification, cancellation, and objection

You may request access, rectification, cancellation, or objection regarding your data, and you may withdraw consent when applicable, by emailing soporte@develandoelcodigomasonico.com.

Include your name, the WhatsApp number used, a clear description of the request, and a way to receive the response. Reasonable additional information may be requested to confirm that you are the data subject, without asking for passwords or verification codes.

10. Restriction and withdrawal

You may stop using Cartes, cancel Cartes Plus renewal from the chat, or request deletion of data that no longer needs to be retained. Some requests may be subject to retention obligations or technical and legal limitations.

11. Minors

Cartes is not intentionally directed at minors. If information about a minor is identified without sufficient authorization, its deletion may be requested.

12. Automated decisions

Cartes generates responses and feedback automatically. These responses may contain errors and do not, by themselves, produce legal effects for the user.

13. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when features, providers, or applicable requirements change. The current version will be identified by its date and made available on this page.

14. Related terms

Use of the service is also governed by the Cartes Terms of Use.
